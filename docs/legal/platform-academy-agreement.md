Between **the Platform** (`{{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}}`, a **natural-person or legal-entity business** licensed in Iran; the owner's or entity's name, national/company ID, economic code and address are published in the site footer and eNamad certificate; if the Platform's legal form changes, the new person succeeds to this Agreement with notice and no loss of the Academy's rights) and **the Academy Manager** (the natural person who creates an academy and accepts electronically, entering their own details and, where applicable, those of the institute or company they represent). ## 2. Nature of the Relationship
SaaS to operate an academy. **The Academy — not the Platform — sells educational services to students**; the Platform is a **technical intermediary and collection agent** receiving student payments on behalf of the Academy and settling **the full amount**. **No commission is taken.** No employment, partnership, agency or general mandate is created. The Platform is not an educational institution and accredits nothing.

## 3. Subscription
Fixed subscription plus optional one-shot capacity packs is the only revenue (**0% commission**). Plan names, monthly and quarterly (discounted) prices and every cap (storage, private-tutoring students) are published on the [pricing page](/pricing); prices exclude VAT; no yearly billing.
**14-day trial:** once per **person** on their first academy, no card required, non-refundable; subdomain only, 1 GB storage; unlocked by the first payment; 5-day window after the trial, then a never-paid academy is deleted with notice.
**Storage** hard-blocks new uploads at 100% (existing files stay readable); buy a storage pack or upgrade — no automatic overage. Public enrollment is unlimited; the only seat is the active private-tutoring student.
Renewals are **initiated by the Academy**, never auto-charged. **Price/plan changes** at the Platform's discretion, effective on publication, never affecting an already-paid period, applying only to the next renewal/purchase; claims waived within Section 9. Mid-period upgrades cost only the daily-rate difference for remaining days; downgrades apply at next renewal. **Subscription fees and packs are non-refundable** (the trial is the evaluation window) except under 10.2.

## 4. Collection, Wallet & Settlement
Full student payments are credited to the Academy wallet; settlement to the Academy's verified IBAN in the registered name after the cooldown, on request. KYC (national ID, birth date, IBAN match) required before first settlement. The Platform may **hold** settlement and **set off** its claims against the wallet on suspicious/disputed/reversed transactions, judicial/AML orders, unpaid fees, damages or Section 8 breach. Negative balance due within 15 days. AML reporting as required by law. All money movement is immutably logged.

## 5. Tax
VAT added at the legal rate on subscription invoices. The Academy is seller of record and bears income tax and VAT on course sales, registers with Moadian if in scope, and indemnifies the Platform for tax claims arising from course sales.

## 6. Refunds
Students have **no refund right** (immediate performance with consent — Article 38(a) of the E-Commerce Act). Any refund is a goodwill decision of the Academy, made **outside the Platform, directly with the student**; the Platform offers no refund tool and pays nothing; subscription fees are never refunded for a student cancellation. Only exception: court/bank-mandated refunds are executed and debited to the Academy.

## 7. Data
Learning record belongs to the Academy and its students; the Platform is processor/custodian. Export (JSON) available at any time, including while locked or suspended. The Academy warrants lawful consent (guardian consent for minors) for every personal datum it enters. Limited technical licence to store, back up, transcode, stream. The Academy keeps its own originals.

## 8. Academy Duties
Verified identity (gates publishing and settlement); publish identity, prices and terms (Article 33); hold all teaching permits — the Platform grants none; pre-publication review of its own and its staff's content; no prohibited content; content ownership warranty; no Platform endorsement; sole criminal/civil answerability of the Academy Manager and duty to defend the Platform; **uncapped indemnity**, recoverable by set-off; student rights and guardian consent; lawful SMS use; recording consent; account security; no reverse engineering, scraping or competing product.

## 9. Platform Liability
Best-effort availability; no indirect/consequential loss, no loss of exportable data; no liability for price changes, Academy conduct, student disputes, gateway/infrastructure/internet outages. **Cap: fees received from the Academy in the prior 3 months** (student funds excluded and always settled). Carve-outs: wilful misconduct/gross negligence and the settlement duty. Confidentiality of Academy business data.

## 10. Suspension & Termination
Termination for breach without notice and without refund. Either party may terminate on 30 days' notice; if the **Platform** terminates without cause, the unused prepaid portion is refunded pro rata; if the Academy does, nothing is refunded. Non-renewal: 5-day read-only window (manager can sign in, export, pay); then locked — day 0–30 everything kept, day 30 uploaded media deleted (learning record kept), day 90 full deletion; renewal restores at the normal price; student access terms are extended by the locked days; the Academy compensates its own students. Export at every stage; after day 90 data is unrecoverable. Notice-and-takedown, no refund for breach suspension, Article 23 retention (traffic ≥6 months, identity ≥6 months after closure).

Force majeure incl. sanctions, national/international internet shutdowns, Shetab/Shaparak or gateway outages, state orders (accrued payments excluded). The Platform may assign to a successor/affiliate/acquirer; the Academy may not assign without consent. Notices via panel/SMS/email are deemed served on dispatch. Entire agreement, severability, no waiver, survival, Persian governs. Iranian law; 30-day negotiation via support; then the competent courts of the Platform's registered seat; system records are admissible under Article 12. Electronic acceptance recorded (version, time, IP, content hash).
