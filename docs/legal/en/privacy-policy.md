# Privacy Policy

> **Version:** 2.0 · **Jurisdiction:** Islamic Republic of Iran · **Governing language:** Persian
>
> Reference mirror of [privacy-policy.md](../privacy-policy.md). The Persian text governs.

## 1. Who Processes Your Data
The Platform (**Mentoma**; registration details in the site footer) processes your data. **For data an Academy enters about its own students, the Academy is legally responsible for collection and consent**; the Platform processes it on the Academy's behalf to provide the service.

## 2. What We Collect
- **Identity:** name, mobile number (primary account identifier), email and, where required, national ID.
- **Academy-manager verification (KYC):** legal/natural name, national ID or company ID, date of birth, national ID card image, address, IBAN and permit declaration — for settlement and for publishing the academy's public site.
- **Educational (learning record):** courses, quizzes, grades, submissions, feedback, live-class attendance, in-course messages and session recordings (if enabled by the Academy).
- **Financial:** payment, invoice and settlement records. **Card details are never stored by us**; payment happens at a licensed bank gateway.
- **Technical:** IP address, time, browser and device type, user ID, request type and path, error and security logs.
- **Support:** ticket text, abuse reports and image attachments.

## 3. Why and On What Basis
Data is processed to **provide the service, verify identity, process payments and settlements, send service SMS (login codes, reminders, notices), support, security and fraud prevention, anonymous statistics to improve the service, and legal obligations (tax, judicial, Article 23 of the Computer Crimes Act)**.

The legal basis is your **express consent** on accepting this policy under **Articles 58 and 59 of the E-Commerce Act**, together with **performance of the contract** and **legal obligation**. For users under 18, guardian consent is required and the enrolling Academy is responsible for obtaining it.

## 4. Ownership of the Learning Record
**The learning record belongs to the Academy and the student**; the Platform is its custodian, does not use it for purposes outside the service, and backs it up continuously.

## 5. Sharing
**Data is never sold or rented.** It is shared only with:
- **your Academy** (its manager and teachers, within their roles);
- **licensed payment gateways**, to execute transactions;
- **contracted infrastructure providers** (hosting and storage inside Iran, SMS delivery, video streaming, live classes, error monitoring) who access only what is needed and under confidentiality obligations;
- the Platform's **legal successor** on a business transfer, bound by the same obligations;
- **judicial, law-enforcement and tax authorities** within legal requirements and on the order of a competent authority.

Primary user data is kept on infrastructure **inside Iran**.

## 6. Retention and Deletion
- Data is kept as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations (e.g. financial and tax records).
- After an academy is locked for non-renewal, uploaded files are deleted on **day 30** and the whole academy — including the learning record — on **day 90**; the Academy may export before then. A trial academy that never paid is deleted 5 days after the trial ends.
- Closed user accounts are kept in a deactivated state so the legally required records below remain attributable.

**Mandatory technical retention (Article 23 of the Computer Crimes Act):** as a hosting provider the Platform keeps **traffic data** — IP address, time, user ID, academy, type and path of state-changing requests and sign-in/out — for **at least 6 months**, and **user identity data** for **at least 6 months after account closure**. Each time an academy site is made public, the actor, time and IP are recorded. **Request bodies, passwords and one-time codes are never stored in these records.** This is a **legal obligation** and is not stopped by an erasure request (Section 8); such records are disclosed only on the order of a competent authority.

## 7. Security
We use encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control, **isolation of each academy's data from every other academy**, logging of support-staff access to data, and regular backups. Platform staff have accepted a separate confidentiality undertaking. No system is 100% secure; in the event of a security breach affecting your data we notify you **as soon as reasonably possible** via the panel, SMS or email.

## 8. Your Rights
- **Access and correction:** most data can be viewed and edited in your panel; the rest through support.
- **Erasure:** you may request account closure and deletion of personal data; deletion is carried out to the extent allowed by legal obligations (Section 6) and the Academy's rights over the learning record.
- **Export:** an Academy can export its full learning record.
- **SMS:** service SMS is necessary to provide the service; unsubscribe from an Academy's promotional SMS through that Academy.

Requests are filed through the support system or `hello@mentoma.ir` and are normally answered within **15 business days**.

## 9. Cookies and Tracking
We use **essential** cookies and local storage for sign-in, security and preferences. Usage statistics are processed in **anonymous/aggregate** form. The Platform does not give your data to advertising networks.

## 10. Changes
The Platform may update this policy. A new version is effective on publication in the service, material changes are announced, and re-acceptance may be required. The current version is always available in the service.
