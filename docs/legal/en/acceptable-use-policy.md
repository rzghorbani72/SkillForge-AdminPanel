## 1. The Platform's Legal Position

1.1. The Platform ("Mentoma") is a **hosting and software service provider**. Content published on
an Academy's public website, courses, classes, quizzes, messages, and uploaded files is
**created and published by the Academy and its users, not by the Platform**.

1.2. **The Platform does not pre-screen, approve, or moderate user content** and has no obligation
to do so. Granting access to the Service is **in no way an approval of content, a verification of
educational claims, or a publishing licence for that content.**

1.3. The absence of pre-screening does not waive the Platform's right to **review, remove, or block
any content at any time** (Section 5).

## 2. Academy and User Content Duties

2.1. The Academy must **review its own content — and that of its teachers, staff, and students —
before publication** for compliance with the laws of the Islamic Republic of Iran.

2.2. The Academy must **own or hold a valid licence** for every item it publishes (text, video,
audio, images, handouts, quizzes, software, fonts) and must produce proof on request.

2.3. **Obtaining the permits its teaching activity requires — from the Technical and Vocational
Training Organization, the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Science, or any other competent
authority — is entirely the Academy's responsibility.** By accepting this policy the Academy
declares it holds, or is in the process of obtaining, every such permit; liability for unlicensed
activity rests solely with the Academy.

2.4. The Academy must give the Platform its **true identity** (legal or natural person name,
national ID / company ID, phone, address) and publish it on its own public website, as required by
Iran's **E-Commerce Act (2003)**. The Platform conditions going live on this information being
complete.

2.5. The Academy must impose these same duties on its **teachers, staff, and agents**, and supervise
them. The Academy is liable for their breaches.

2.6. The Academy warrants that for every personal datum it enters about a student (name, mobile
number, age, grades) it holds **the data subject's lawful consent — and, for anyone under 18, the
guardian's** (Articles 58 and 59 of the E-Commerce Act), and that it has obtained participants'
consent before **recording live classes**.

## 3. Prohibited Content

Publishing, uploading, transmitting, or making available the following through the Platform is
**strictly prohibited**. The list follows **Article 21 of Iran's Computer Crimes Act** and the
rulings of the **Committee for Determining Instances of Criminal Content**, and is **not
exhaustive** — any other criminal content is equally prohibited.

- **(a) Against public decency:** obscene or indecent material; incitement to vice or sexual
  deviance; instrumental use of persons in imagery; unlicensed matchmaking services.
- **(b) Against Islamic sanctities:** atheistic or anti-Islamic content; insult to Islam, the
  Prophets, the Imams, Imam Khomeini, the Supreme Leader, or the sources of emulation; propaganda
  for deviant sects.
- **(c) Against public security:** organizing to disturb national security; bomb threats;
  propaganda against the Islamic Republic; incitement to ethnic or racial division; incitement of
  the armed forces to mutiny; disclosure of classified state, military, or judicial documents;
  spreading falsehood and public alarm.
- **(d) Against officials and public bodies:** insult, satire, defamation, or falsehood against
  state officials, institutions, and public organizations.
- **(e) Computer-crime-related:** tools and data made for committing computer crimes; unauthorized
  sale of passwords or access data; tutorials on unauthorized access, interception, or data
  tampering; **circumvention tools and filtering-bypass tutorials**; **pyramid schemes** and
  **gambling**; forgery of bank or government sites; **phishing**; deceptive tags and pop-ups.
- **(f) Incitement to crime:** incitement to violence or suicide; promotion of narcotics,
  psychotropics, or tobacco; links to blocked sites or banned publications; facilitation of public
  offences (bribery, embezzlement, fraud, smuggling); promotion of extravagance; sale or instruction
  on satellite reception equipment; unlicensed sale of weapons, munitions, explosive, chemical,
  radioactive, or microbial materials, anaesthetic gases, and stun devices; unlicensed internet
  radio/TV broadcasting.
- **(g) Audiovisual works and intellectual property:** commercial distribution of audiovisual works
  without a Ministry of Culture licence; passing off unlicensed works as licensed; **infringement of
  intellectual property** — including uploading or selling another party's course, book, handout,
  software, font, or video without the owner's written permission; unlicensed or criminal video
  games.
- **(h) Election-related criminal content:** encouraging an election boycott or reduced turnout;
  false claims about results, candidate qualifications, or withdrawals; insult or defamation against
  candidates and electoral bodies; campaigning outside the legal window; use of state resources for
  campaigning; vote buying.
- **(i) Platform-specific prohibitions:**
  - **Educational fraud** — selling a course or credential that is not delivered, claiming false
    accreditation, or guaranteeing unrealistic outcomes (guaranteed exam pass or employment).
  - **Misleading certificates** — issuing a certificate falsely presented as governmental,
    academic, or international.
  - **Restricted goods and services** — medicines, supplements, medical, psychological, or
    counselling services without the licence of the Food and Drug Administration, the Psychology and
    Counselling Organization, or other competent authority.
  - **Violation of personal dignity** — publishing another person's private data or images without
    consent; insult or defamation of natural or legal persons.
  - **Messaging abuse** — bulk promotional SMS to people who are not the Academy's students, or any
    message containing content prohibited above.
  - **Infrastructure abuse** — using Platform storage as a general file host unrelated to teaching,
    crypto mining, spam, or attacking other systems.
  - **Unlicensed financial or migration services** — promoting or teaching investment, FX/crypto
    trading, loans or migration with promised returns or guaranteed outcomes, or without the
    relevant authority's licence.
  - **Fabricated or manipulated media** — synthetic images, audio or video of real people
    (deepfakes), or content falsely attributed to a person or institution.
  - **Content unsuitable for minors** — adult or violent material in courses that have students
    under 18, or any contact with under-18 students outside the teaching environment.
  - **Circumventing the Platform** — acquiring students through the Platform and moving them
    elsewhere to escape these duties, or using the Platform to move funds unrelated to a genuine
    educational service.

## 5. Review, Removal, and Suspension (Notice and Takedown)

5.1. The Platform may, **at any time, without prior notice and without liability**, remove or
disable any content it reasonably deems in breach of this policy, and suspend the Academy's public
website or its entire account.

5.2. On the order of a **judicial, law-enforcement, security authority, or the Committee for
Determining Instances of Criminal Content**, the Platform will act **immediately and without prior
notice** to remove content or suspend the account, and will provide the required information to that
authority within the limits of legal obligation.

5.3. **No fee is refunded for a suspension caused by a breach**, and the subscription term is not
extended.

5.4. For a serious or repeated breach, the Platform may **terminate the agreement immediately** and
**refuse any future dealings** with that person or Academy.

5.5. **Reporting abuse:** anyone — including people without an account — may report violating
content through the **report form on any academy site**, the Platform's support system, or
`info@mentoma.ir`. Every report is recorded with a **reference and a review deadline**, and is
reviewed within **72 business hours** (Saturday–Wednesday). On a **judicial order** the Platform acts
**immediately**, without waiting for that deadline.

## 6. Liability, Answerability, and Indemnity

6.1. **Criminal and civil liability for content rests entirely with the Academy and the publishing
user.**

6.2. **Answering to judicial, law-enforcement, security, and governmental authorities regarding
content is the Academy's responsibility**, not the Platform's.

6.3. The Academy shall **fully indemnify** the Platform for any damage, fine, litigation cost, or
legal fee arising from a breach of this policy, and hold the Platform **harmless** against any
third-party claim.

6.4. The liability disclaimer and liability cap in Section 9 of the Service Agreement **do not apply
to claims under this Section**; the Academy's indemnity obligation is uncapped and the Platform may
**set it off** against the Academy's wallet balance.

6.5. If a claim is brought against the Platform over the Academy's content or activity, the Academy
must **join the proceedings, defend the Platform** and bear the costs.

## 7. Data and Log Retention

7.1. Under **Article 23 of the Computer Crimes Act**, the Platform, as a hosting service provider,
retains **traffic data** for **at least 6 months**. That data covers the **IP address, timestamp,
user identifier, academy, request method and path**, and is recorded for **state-changing requests**
(create, edit, delete) and for sign-in/sign-out. **Request bodies, query strings, passwords and
one-time codes are never retained.**

7.1.1. **User identity information** is retained for at least **6 months after the account closes**;
deleted accounts are soft-deleted so traffic records remain attributable to a person.

7.2. Each time an Academy's website is **made public**, the Platform writes an append-only record of
the **acting user, timestamp, IP address, and the Academy's declared legal identity at that moment**.
This record survives deletion of the Academy and is made available to competent authorities within the
limits of legal obligation.

7.3. This retention is a **legal obligation**; a personal-data deletion request does not stop it.
