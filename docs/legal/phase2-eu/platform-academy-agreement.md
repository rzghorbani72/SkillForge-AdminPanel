## 4. Subscription Fees

The Platform charges a **fixed monthly subscription**, or optional **3-month prepaid at 3× the monthly price (no discount)**, and takes **no commission on student enrollments (0%)**. Payments collected from students are remitted to the Academy **in full**; the subscription is invoiced to the Academy separately. Soft storage overage on renewals is not used — uploads stop at the included quota (plus any purchased storage add-on packs).

Plan tiers, prices, and included storage are stated on the pricing page and in the Academy's subscription dashboard.

- **Price and plan changes:** the Platform may, **at its sole discretion and at any time, without prior
  notice**, change subscription prices, storage quotas, or the composition/features of any plan;
  introduce new plans; or discontinue or merge an existing plan. A change takes effect from the moment
  it is published on the pricing page. An **already-paid, confirmed period is never repriced** and runs
  to completion under the terms in force when it was paid; a change applies only to the **next renewal
  or a new purchase/upgrade**. Continued use, or any renewal/purchase after the effective date,
  constitutes acceptance. The Academy **waives any claim, complaint, or demand for damages** arising
  from such changes, subject to the liability cap in Section 7.

- **Free trial:** one month of full access, **once per person** (not per academy), on their first academy. Nothing is charged, so the trial is not refundable. During the trial the academy runs on the platform subdomain only (no custom domain) and uploads are capped at 1 GB; both unlock on the first payment.
- **Storage overage:** each plan includes a storage quota. Usage beyond the quota is billed per GB **on the subscription renewal invoice only**, never deducted from student payments.
- **Non-renewal:** the Academy gets a **5-day payment window** in which it is **read-only** — teachers and students lose access while the manager may still sign in, view and export data, and pay. After that it is locked.
- **Deletion — trial:** a trial academy that **never paid** is deleted with its data after the payment window, to free storage; notice is given first.
- **Deletion — former payers:** once locked, the Academy is wound down in stages measured from the lock date. **Days 0–30:** everything retained. **Day 30:** uploaded files (video, image, audio, documents) are deleted to free storage, while courses, students and the **learning record (grades, quizzes, submissions, feedback, attendance) are kept**. **Day 90:** full deletion, and the slug and domain are released.
- **Reactivation:** renewing before day 90 restores full access at the **normal subscription price — no charge applies for the lock period**. Deleted files can be re-uploaded from the Academy's own originals.
- **Export:** the Academy may download its complete learning record at any stage, including while locked. Notice with export instructions precedes each deletion step.
- The Platform may grant a longer payment deadline at its discretion.
