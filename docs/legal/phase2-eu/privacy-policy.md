## 3. Your Rights under GDPR

You have the right to:
- **Access** — request a copy of your personal data.
- **Rectification** — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
- **Erasure** — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
- **Portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Restriction** — ask us to pause processing while a dispute is resolved.
- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Withdraw consent** — where processing is consent-based, withdraw at any time.

To exercise any right, email: privacy@[domain]. We will respond within 30 days.

## 4. Data Retention

| Data type | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Account data | Duration of contract + 3 years |
| Financial/invoice data | 7 years (Estonian Accounting Act) |
| Learning records | Duration of contract + 1 year (exportable) |
| Support tickets | 2 years |
| Deleted account data | Purged within 30 days of termination |
| Traffic logs (IP, timestamp, user id, event) | 6 months — legal obligation |
| Publisher record per published item (id, time, IP) | 6 months after account closure — legal obligation |

Traffic and publisher logs are kept to meet hosting-provider record-keeping duties and to answer
lawful requests. Because the basis is a legal obligation, an erasure request under Section 3 does not
stop this retention; the logs are disclosed only within the limits of that obligation.
