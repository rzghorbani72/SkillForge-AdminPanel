# Refund Policy

**Effective date:** 2025-01-01  
**Governing law:** Republic of Estonia (EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU)

## 1. Statutory Right of Withdrawal (14-day Cooling-off)

If you are a consumer (natural person acting outside a trade or profession) based in the EU, you have the right to withdraw from a purchase contract within **14 calendar days** without giving any reason ("cooling-off period").

The 14-day period begins from the day you complete your purchase.

**Exception:** If you explicitly request that the digital content service begins immediately and acknowledge that this waives your right of withdrawal, the cooling-off right is lost once access to the content is granted.

## 2. How to Request a Refund

To exercise your right of withdrawal, notify us before the 14-day period expires by:
- Emailing support@[domain] with subject "Refund Request — [Order ID]", or
- Using the refund request form in your account dashboard.

We will refund the full purchase price within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal notice, using the same payment method you used.

## 3. Refunds for Subscription Plans

Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled at any time; the cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. We do not issue pro-rata refunds for partial months unless required by applicable law.

## 4. Refunds Outside the Cooling-off Period

After the 14-day cooling-off period, refunds are at our discretion. We will consider refund requests in cases of:
- Significant technical failures that prevented access to paid content.
- Duplicate charges.

## 5. Non-refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refund once consumed:
- Completed live sessions attended by the student.
- Downloaded certificates.
